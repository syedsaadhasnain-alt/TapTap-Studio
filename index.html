The Game uses the device’s Advertising ID for showing personalized and non-personalized ads through Google AdMob. Users can reset or disable the Advertising ID from their device settings at any time.

D. Location Information

We do not collect or access precise GPS location.

However, Firebase Analytics and AdMob may automatically collect approximate location (such as country or region) based on the User's IP address. This data is non-precise, cannot identify an exact location, and is used only for analytics and ad relevance.

We do not directly collect personal information such as names, emails, or addresses for general gameplay.

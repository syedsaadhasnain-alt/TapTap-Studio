Privacy Policy for Poopin Cube

This Privacy Policy governs how TapTap Studio ("We," "Us," or "Our") collects, uses, and protects information from users ("Users") of the mobile game Poopin Cube (the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

A. Device and App Information (Required for Gameplay & Ads)

To provide a functional gaming experience and support in-game features, our third-party SDKs may collect:

  • Installed Application Metadata: A list of applications installed on your device. This is used by our advertising partners for attribution (to ensure you don't see ads for games you already have), analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Technical Data: Device model, OS version, Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and crash logs.

B. Third-Party Services Used

We use trusted third-party SDKs that may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Unity Ads: Used to display advertisements and personalize ad content.
  • IronSource: Our mediation platform used to manage and optimize ad delivery.
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to collect usage statistics and performance data to improve the Game.
  • Google Play Services: Used for achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves.

C. Advertising ID Usage

The Game uses the device’s Advertising ID for showing personalized and non-personalized ads. Users can reset or disable the Advertising ID from their device settings (Android Settings > Google > Ads) at any time.

D. Location Information

We do not collect or access precise GPS location. However, our partners may collect approximate location (country/region) based on IP addresses for analytics and ad relevance.

2. How We Use Collected Information

  • To Improve the Game: Identify bugs, optimize gameplay, and enhance performance.
  • Analytics: Understand player behavior and geographic market performance.
  • Advertising & Fraud Prevention: Deliver relevant ads and ensure device integrity to prevent automated bot activity.
  • Functionality: Enable leaderboards and cloud saves via Google Play Services.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent Users’ personal information. We share aggregated, non-personal data and device identifiers with our trusted partners (Google, Unity, and IronSource) for the purposes of advertising, analytics, and security compliance.

4. Children's Privacy

This Game is intended for a general audience (13+). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the Game indicates acceptance of any changes.
